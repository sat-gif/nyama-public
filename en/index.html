How it works

Three moves, and nothing gets lost

Capture, organise, cook. Everything else in Nyama exists to serve those three.

1

Share it. It's filed.

A TikTok video, an Instagram post, a blog that never ends, a photo of a cookbook page, a message your mother sent you. You share it to Nyama, and the recipe comes out clean: ingredients on one side, steps on the other.

You didn't retype a thing. It takes seconds.

  • From the «Share» button in any app
  • A photo of a book or a card: the text is read for you
  • A pasted link, pasted text, or by hand if you prefer
  • You read it over, fix whatever you like, and save
2

In three years, it'll still be there.

Every recipe joins your library, with its tags and its collections. You search «lemon chicken», you find it. No feed, no algorithm, none of other people's recipes.

Only yours. Filed the way you file things.

  • A search that digs through titles and ingredients alike
  • Collections: «quick ones», «for having people over», «what the kids will actually eat»
  • Filters by mealtime, by time, by mood
  • Your recipes stay on your phone, readable even with no signal
3

Hands covered in flour, the screen keeping up.

Cook mode shows one step at a time, large, and the screen doesn't go dark at the worst moment. You tick off ingredients as you go. You never lose your place again.

Cooking goes back to being the good part, not an obstacle course.

  • One step per screen, in large type
  • The ingredients for that step right in front of you
  • The screen stays on for the whole cook
  • Quantities adjust to how many you're feeding

And then

What turns a library into dinner

A well-filed recipe is good. A week of meals already decided is better.

Shopping list

Your recipes become a list sorted by aisle, quantities added up. You tick items off one-handed at the shop.

The week, decided

You drop your meals onto the days ahead. On Tuesday evening you stop opening the fridge hoping for an idea.

Adapt a recipe

Dairy-free, for six instead of four, a little lighter. The recipe rewrites itself and you keep the version that suits you.

Calories

What's actually on your plate, estimated for you. No calculator, nothing to retype.

Offline

Your recipes live on your phone. In a kitchen with no signal, at your parents', on holiday: they're there.

Honest about its mistakes

When the automatic reading isn't sure of itself, Nyama tells you instead of pretending. Two taps and it's fixed.

Questions

What people ask us

Does it really work with a TikTok or Instagram video?

When the recipe is written in the caption or the description — which it usually is — Nyama reads it and files it. When it only exists spoken aloud in the video, Nyama says so plainly and lets you fill in the rest, rather than inventing it.

What if the recipe comes out wrong?

Nyama flags the parts it isn't sure about instead of acting as if nothing happened. You read it over, fix it in two taps, and save. A wrong recipe in your library is worse than a missing one.

Do my recipes go to a server?

Your recipes live on your phone. What does go to a server, and only in those cases, is spelled out in the privacy policy. No selling your data, no advertising.

Do I have to create an account?

Yes, with Apple or Google, in a few seconds. That's what keeps your recipes yours. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, without writing to us.

Is it available on Android?

Not yet. Nyama is starting on iPhone. Android will follow if enough people ask for it.

What happens if I stop Nyama Plus?

You keep every recipe. You simply go back to the free limits for whatever you add next. Nothing is deleted.
