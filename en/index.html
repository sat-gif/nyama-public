What people ask us.

Does it work with all my sources?

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and its Shorts, Facebook, Pinterest, cooking sites and blogs, a photo of a cookbook page, pasted text, a note or a message: Nyama reads what's written and files it away. And when the recipe is written nowhere — when it only exists spoken in the video — Nyama listens to it and reconstructs it. If it isn't sure, it tells you rather than inventing.

What if the recipe comes out wrong?

Nyama flags the parts it isn't sure about instead of acting as if nothing happened. You read it over, fix it in two taps, and save. A wrong recipe in your library is worse than a missing one.

Do my recipes go to a server?

Your recipes live on your phone. What does go to a server, and only in those cases, is spelled out in the privacy policy. No selling your data.

Do I have to create an account?

It does one thing: it keeps your recipes when a phone breaks or gets replaced. So yes, at the end of the walkthrough — with Apple or Google, in a few seconds, no password to remember. You can delete your account and everything in it from the app, without writing to us.

Is it available on Android?

Not yet. Nyama is starting on iPhone; the Android version is on its way.

What happens if I stop Nyama Plus?

You keep every recipe. You simply fall back on the free allowance for whatever you add next: 5 recipes a week, topped up every Monday. Nothing is deleted.
